New manpower request
Ping all directors with a manpower need. If all decline within 48 hours, you'll get an email to call the union hall.
Polling flow
1. Request is sent to all directors
2. Directors respond Yes (can spare someone) or No
3. First Yes → request approved, director coordinates
4. All No within 48 hours → email requester to call the union
New labor transfer
